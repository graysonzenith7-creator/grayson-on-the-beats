🧑‍🏫 Vocal Coach

  • Warm up before difficult notes.
  • Keep your jaw and shoulders relaxed.
  • Use breath support rather than squeezing your throat.
  • Record one chorus softly, one normally and one with performance energy.
  • Choose the take with the best emotion and timing — not simply the loudest.
⚠️ Never force a painful note. If your voice hurts, stop and rest.
