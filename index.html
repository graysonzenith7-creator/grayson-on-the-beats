🧑‍🏫 15-Minute Vocal Warm-Up

  1. 2 min slow breathing.
  2. 3 min humming/lip trills.
  3. 3 min gentle sirens.
  4. 3 min vowel clarity: ah–eh–ee–oh–oo.
  5. 4 min chorus delivery at comfortable volume.
Professional rule: never push through throat pain or persistent hoarseness.
